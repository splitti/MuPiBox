# MuPiBox Manual

The MuPiBox is a music and audiobook player for children, which they operate themselves on a touch screen. This manual belongs to the box and is stored on it: you can always reach it at `http://<address of the box>/manual/`, even without internet.

> [!TIP]
> Press [[/]] to jump to the search at the top. It searches the whole manual, including the settings reference.

## Two interfaces

The box has two interfaces that work together:

- **The display** on the box. This is where the child listens: tap an audiobook or a song, done. Chapter: [Using the display](bedienung/index.md).
- **The app** in the browser of a phone or PC. This is where parents set everything up: content, times, volume, Wi-Fi, updates. Chapter: [Opening the app and signing in](erste-schritte/app-oeffnen.md).

## How this manual is written

- I write menu paths like this: **Settings › Audio › Volume**. That is the way through the app.
- Boxes marked **Warning** warn about things that can break something or delete data.

For help beyond this manual there is the website [mupibox.de](https://mupibox.de) and the community's [Discord channel](https://discord.gg/4EjCgpCbbe).
