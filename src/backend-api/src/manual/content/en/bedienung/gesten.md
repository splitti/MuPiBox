# Gestures and full screen

On the pages with covers (start page, lists, player, Continue) the **top bar** can be folded away. The covers then have the whole screen and are larger. In this full-screen mode the child operates the box with swipe gestures.

![Full screen: without the top bar, the covers are larger](display-fullscreen.png)

| Gesture | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Swipe two fingers up** | hides the bar |
| **Swipe two fingers down** | shows the bar again |
| **Swipe one finger up from the bottom edge** | one level back, like the back arrow in the bar |
| **Swipe two fingers from the right edge to the left** | next category (start page only) |
| **Swipe two fingers from the left edge to the right** | previous category (start page only) |

> [!TIP]
> The categories run in a circle: from the last category the swipe leads to the first and the other way round.

## What changes in full screen

- The two-finger swipes work the same on **all** pages with covers. Once the bar is hidden, it stays hidden on all levels, also after a page change or reload.
- The covers get larger: in Cover Flow 350 × 350 pixels, with a little space at the top. The cover in the player also sits at the same height as in the overview.
- The reflection under the cover reaches the bottom edge of the screen.
- The category tabs and the back arrow are in the bar and so cannot be tapped. The swipe gestures are there for that.

> [!NOTE]
> The one-finger swipe from the bottom edge only works with the bar hidden and not on the start page, because there is no level above it. It starts in a narrow strip at the bottom of the screen.

## Press and hold

Two more gestures are hold gestures and can be adjusted (**Settings › Display & controls › Controls on the display**):

- **Hold the cover in the player**: opens the track list ([The player](player.md)).
- **Hold the status indicator**: shows the QR code for parents ([Settings on the display](eltern-zugang.md)).
