# The start page

The start page shows the content of the box, sorted into categories. The categories are tabs at the top of the display, each with a symbol:

![The start page with the Audiobooks category](display-home.png)

| Category | Symbol | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Audiobooks** | book | audiobooks and radio plays |
| **Music** | notes | music |
| **NAS** | server | folders from the network drive that are not assigned to another category ([NAS](../inhalte/nas.md)) |
| **Radio & podcasts** | radio waves | radio stations and podcasts |

When you add content you decide which category it goes into ([Adding content](../inhalte/index.md)). Folders from the NAS can also be in Audiobooks, Music or Radio & podcasts. If all shown NAS folders are in one of these categories, the NAS tab goes away.

![The Music category](display-music.png)

## How the covers are arranged

- After installation the **cover flow view** is on: one large cover lies in the middle, its neighbours are smaller. Swipe sideways to move the list. If you tap a cover at the edge, it moves to the middle; the middle one opens with a tap.
- If you switch it off, three covers stand side by side, the name below. Swipe sideways to page on. A tap on a cover opens it.
- The theme **coverflow** has an arrangement of its own: the covers are tilted, only the middle one faces front. Here too a cover at the edge first moves to the middle.

Under **Settings › Appearance › View** you set:

- **Cover flow view (stage)**: on (default) or off, for all themes except coverflow,
- **Read the name aloud when the cover stops**: only with the cover flow view (voice and language: **Audio › Speech output**),
- **Show folder and album names**: only with the theme coverflow,
- **Scroll bar below the covers**: **Default** (as the theme has it), **Full width** (across the whole width and thicker, easy to hit with big fingers) or **Hide**. You can drag the bar, also in the cover flow view: the covers follow, and on release the nearest one snaps into place,
- **Header with background** and **Player with background panel**: only for the older plain themes (such as blue, green, red, dark). Switched off, the header lies on the background without a coloured band, and the player's buttons stand without a panel, in the colours of the header,
- **Full screen by swipe gesture**: only with the theme coverflow, see below.

## Full screen in the theme coverflow

**Swiping up with two fingers** hides the header and makes the covers bigger. **Two fingers down** bring it back. Without the header:

- **one finger from the bottom edge upwards**: one level back (like the back button),
- **two fingers from the right or left edge sideways**: the next or previous category.

The box remembers whether the header is hidden. If you switch **Full screen by swipe gesture** off, the header is back at once. Zooming with two fingers is always off on the box.

## Folders and albums

Some entries are folders, for example an artist or a series of radio plays. A tap opens the folder and shows its albums. The back arrow at the top left goes up one level.

## Hiding tabs

If you have no NAS content, for example, hide the tab: **Settings › Display & controls › Controls on the display › Tabs on the display**. Every tab has a switch, on means visible. The visible tabs share the width. At least one stays visible.

## Resume

The clock symbol to the left of the tabs opens the **Resume** list with the most recently played items, so the child carries on listening exactly where they stopped. How many entries it has is set under **Controls on the display › Continue listening › Entries under “Resume”**. More: [Continue and “Now playing”](fortsetzen.md).
