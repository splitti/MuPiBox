# The start page

The start page shows the content of the box, sorted into categories. The categories are tabs at the top of the display:

![The start page with the Audiobooks category](display-home.png)

| Category | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Audiobooks** | audiobooks and radio plays |
| **Music** | music |
| **NAS** | media from the network drive ([NAS](../inhalte/nas.md)) |
| **Radio & podcasts** | radio stations and podcasts |

When you add content you decide which category it goes into ([Adding content](../inhalte/index.md)).

![The Music category](display-music.png)

## Cover Flow and grid

With Cover Flow, one large cover lies in the middle, its neighbours are smaller and tilted. Swipe sideways to move the list and tap the middle cover to open it. If you tap a cover at the edge, it first moves to the middle.

Under **Settings › Appearance › View** you set:

- whether the Cover Flow view is on,
- whether the box **reads out the name** when a cover stops in the middle (voice and language: **Audio › Speech output**),
- whether folder and album names are shown,
- whether the horizontal scroll bar is hidden.

## Folders and albums

Some entries are folders, for example an artist or a series of radio plays. A tap opens the folder and shows its albums. The back arrow in the bar (or the swipe gesture, see [Gestures and full screen](gesten.md)) goes up one level.

## Hiding categories

If you have no NAS content, for example, hide the category: **Settings › Display & controls › Controls on the display › Hide categories**. The remaining tabs then spread out evenly. At least one category stays visible.

## Continue

Next to the categories there is the **Continue** list with the most recently played items, so the child carries on listening exactly where they stopped. How many entries it has is set under **Controls on the display**. More: [Continue and “Now playing”](fortsetzen.md).
