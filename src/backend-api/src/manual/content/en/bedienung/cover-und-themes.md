# Covers and themes

## Themes

The **theme** determines colours, backgrounds and fonts of the display. There are 67 themes, from plain colours (light, dark, blue, red …) through children's worlds (space, underwater, fire brigade, horse farm …) to character themes. You choose it under **Settings › Appearance › Theme**; after the change the display reloads.

![A selection of the themes](Themes.png)

### Custom theme

The theme **custom** shows a background picture of your own. Upload it under **Settings › Appearance › Custom theme**. A JPG of 800 × 480 pixels fits best, the display's default resolution.

### Boot and maintenance screens

Under **Settings › Appearance › Boot and maintenance screens** you choose the boot screen (15 scenes, as a fixed one or random at every start) and the **maintenance screen**. It appears during an update, during installation, when a new Wi-Fi is set up, when switching off and when the battery is empty. A preview shows beforehand what the picture looks like for each occasion.

Under **Own pictures** you can use your own photo or logo instead of the ready-made scenes for the boot, goodbye and battery-empty pictures. The pictures are generated on the box after saving and can be seen from the next start.

### Text on the display

What the child reads when the limit is reached, a quiet time is running or the QR code for parents appears, you change under **Settings › Appearance › Text on the display**. If a field stays empty, the text of the set language applies.

## Covers

Every album, podcast and radio station shows a **cover**. Where it comes from:

1. a picture that you uploaded yourself or gave as an address,
2. the picture that lies in the folder (`cover.jpg` or similar) or is embedded in the file,
3. for Spotify the cover from Spotify,
4. for albums without a picture of their own, if you want, an **online cover** from iTunes or Deezer ([Covers](../inhalte/cover.md)).

### Missing cover

If the box finds no picture for an entry, it shows a **coloured card with the folder name** in the middle instead. The name is large and wraps onto several lines if it is long. The colour is a gradient from dark to light and is chosen from a palette per folder name: the same folder always has the same colour, different folders look different. The same card appears in the player and in the track list.

![An entry without a cover: a card with the folder name](display-placeholder.png)

If you want a picture instead of the card, upload a cover ([Covers](../inhalte/cover.md)).
