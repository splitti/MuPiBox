# Media control

The extension **Media control** replaces the keys of the player with **one round control**: volume, track change and play/pause are on one disc, and a ring around it fills as the track plays. It sets up nothing on the box and needs no accessories. When it is off, the normal keys are back.

After [installing](installieren.md) it, its settings are in the details of its card under **Settings › System › Extensions**.

## How to use it

| What you do | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| tap **VOL+** at the top / **VOL−** at the bottom | louder / quieter; held down it goes on, like a button on a steering wheel |
| tap the middle | play / pause |
| tap left / right | the track before / after |
| **pull** left / right sideways | winding: a short pull goes to **2×**, a longer one to **4×**. Let go and the key springs back and the winding ends |
| pull **down** while winding | the winding **latches** and goes on at the chosen speed; one tap on the key releases it (a small padlock shows that it is latched) |
| tap or drag the **ring** | choose a place in the track |
| **hold** the middle | the second set: back, eject, shuffle, repeat |

With a radio station the track keys, the winding and the ring do nothing. Where the player cannot choose a place in the track, the keys do not wind.

## The second set

Hold the middle (1, 2 or 3 seconds, see below) and the four outer keys change:

- top **back**: back to the normal keys (this also happens by itself after about 12 seconds without a tap)
- bottom **eject**: opens the tray of the CD drive, only with the extension [USB CD drive](cd-laufwerk.md)
- left **shuffle** and right **repeat** (all, one track, off): where the player can do it; below each is **ON** or **OFF** (for repeat **ALL**, **ONE** or **OFF**)

## Settings

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Design** | **Metal** (a black disc in a frame in the theme's colour), **Colourful (kids)** (a rainbow frame and coloured keys) or **Neon (teenagers)** (black glass with glowing edges). Only colours and looks change, the functions stay the same |
| **Colour of the progress ring** | **To match the design** or a colour from the list, the **colour of the theme** or the **background of the player** (lightened so the ring can be seen). The colour also holds for "ON" in the second set and the winding step |
| **Hold play/pause for the second set** | how long the middle has to be held: 1, 2 or 3 seconds |

A change takes effect at once; the display reloads briefly for it.

> [!NOTE]
> For albums of the box the winding really plays faster (forward) or runs back silently. With Spotify, which cannot play faster, the place moves on in small steps. Without Media control the normal keys wind differently, see [The player](../bedienung/player.md).
