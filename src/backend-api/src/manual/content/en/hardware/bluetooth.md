# Bluetooth

The box can play over Bluetooth on headphones or speakers. Everything about it is under **Settings › Audio › Bluetooth**.

## Switching on

The **Bluetooth** switch turns the box's Bluetooth chip on. If it is off, the chip is off, which saves power. Below it is the address of the box's Bluetooth controller.

## Pairing a device

1. Put the device (headphones, speaker) into **pairing mode**.
2. In the app, under **Pair new devices**, tap **Search**. The search takes about 10 seconds.
3. Choose the device from the list. It then appears under **Paired devices**.

## Connect automatically

**Connect automatically** helps if known devices do not connect by themselves after the box has been switched on.

## Which device does the box play on?

In the player the **headphone icon** chooses the output device: the box itself or a paired Bluetooth device ([The player](../bedienung/player.md)). If the device is not reachable, the box says so instead of simply staying silent.

## Volume with Bluetooth

For Bluetooth devices there is a **maximum of its own**. That way headphones may stay quieter than a speaker. You set it in the app under **Settings › Audio › Volume** ([Sound and volume](sound.md)).
