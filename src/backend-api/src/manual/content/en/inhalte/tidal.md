# TIDAL

With a **TIDAL subscription** the box can play audiobooks, albums and playlists from TIDAL. It plays them itself, like content from the NAS; your phone does not need to be nearby.

> [!WARNING]
> The connection is **unofficial**: the box signs in to TIDAL the way TIDAL's own apps do (with the free library python-tidal). TIDAL does not expressly allow this and may block the connection or, at worst, the account. Use at your own risk. The box only plays tracks TIDAL delivers unencrypted; it does not get around any copy protection.

## Switching on and signing in

You find the page under **Settings › Services › TIDAL**.

1. Switch on **Use TIDAL** and confirm the notice. The first time, the box sets up the connection; this takes about a minute and needs internet.
2. Tap **Connect to TIDAL**. The box shows a **code**.
3. Open **link.tidal.com** on your phone or PC, sign in with your TIDAL account and confirm the code. Your TIDAL password never reaches the box.

The page then shows **Connected to TIDAL** and the account's country. The sign-in is kept on the box only and stays after a restart or an update. **Disconnect** deletes it there; switching off stops the connection without deleting the sign-in.

## Adding content

Under **Library › Add › Search on TIDAL** you search for audiobooks, albums, artists and playlists; **All**, **Artist**, **Albums** and **Playlists** filter the results. What is in the library already has a tick.

- Tap an **album** or a **playlist**, choose the category (Audiobook, Music or Radio & podcasts) and tap **Add**.
- An **artist** shows their albums, EPs and singles to add one by one; a search field, **All**, **Albums**, **EPs & singles** and the **Order** help with many albums. **Add the whole artist** creates one entry for all of their albums: the box and the app show it as a folder with all albums, including ones released later. **Back to search** returns to the results.

TIDAL lists some albums several times (in different qualities); the box shows each one once.

For a whole artist, set a range in the **Library** (tap the entry) under **Episodes and albums**, **Episode from** and **Episode to**: “episode” is the position by release date, 1 is the oldest album. Switch off single albums there with their switch. **Save** applies both; the box and the app then show only these albums.

The entry then appears on the display in the chosen category, and in the app's library with the source **TIDAL**. There you can edit or delete it like any other entry ([Managing content](verwalten.md)).

## Playing

On the display a TIDAL album plays like an album from the memory card: with the track list (long-press the cover), next and previous, seeking, and **continue listening** where it stopped. From the app you start it under **Listen**. Playtime and quiet times apply to TIDAL too.

> [!NOTE]
> TIDAL needs internet. Without internet the box hides the TIDAL entries, like Spotify and radio. With TIDAL switched off, the display and **Listen** do not show them; in the **Library** they stay, to edit or delete. With the box signed out, the entries do not play.
