# Audible

If you bought audiobooks and audio plays on **Audible**, the box plays them from your own Audible library – with covers, chapters and, if you like, the listening position of your account. The box needs internet for this.

> [!WARNING]
> This connection is **unofficial**: Audible has no public interface. The box uses the one of the Audible app and only plays what Audible hands out for streaming; no copy protection is touched. It may stop working at any time. Use at your own risk. Audible is off by default.

## Connecting

The page is under **Settings › Services › Audible**. An assistant guides you through two steps:

1. **Store:** Choose the Audible store your audiobooks were bought in. Austria and Switzerland use audible.de.
2. **Sign in:** **Open Amazon sign-in** opens Amazon's sign-in page in your browser. Sign in there with the account whose audiobooks the box should play – also with the verification code if Amazon asks for it. If Amazon offers to set up a passkey, choose **Not now** or **Skip**. At the end Amazon shows an empty page or "page not found" – that is correct. Copy the **complete address** from the address bar (it contains "maplanding"), paste it into the app and tap **Connect**.

On a phone there is not always an address bar (for example in the app on the home screen): then copy the address via **Share › Copy**. If the Amazon app opens instead of the sign-in page, open the sign-in in the browser or sign the box in on a computer. A sign-in that was started waits for 15 minutes, even if you leave the app in between.

Your password and the verification code stay with Amazon. The box only gets a one-time code and signs in with it as a **device of its own**; in your Amazon account it is listed under **Manage Your Content and Devices** as "…'s Audible for iPhone". It only keeps the key of this device, encrypted on the box.

After that, the page shows the store, the account and the device.

- **Use Audible** switches Audible off without deleting the sign-in. Entries from Audible then stay in the library but are not shown and not played.
- **Sign out** removes the box's device from your Amazon account – only this one, never your other devices. The entries stay in the library and play again after signing in again. If Amazon cannot be reached at that moment, remove the device yourself under **Manage Your Content and Devices** if needed.

## Adding content

Under **Library › Add › From Audible** you find the audiobooks of your account (not podcasts from Audible). The search field filters by title, author, narrator and series; **Reload** fetches the library anew – for example right after a purchase. Otherwise the box uses the state of the last 30 minutes.

- **Audiobooks:** Tap a book, choose the category and **Add**. If it belongs to a series, it is placed in the series' folder on the display, otherwise in the author's.
- **Series:** A series shows its books in the order of the series. **Add the whole series** creates one entry for all of its books in your account – including ones you buy later. The box and the app show it as a folder, the books in the order of the series.

Titles from a subscription (Audible Plus) only play while they are in your library.

## Playing

An audiobook plays like an album: its **chapters are the tracks** – with a chapter list, previous and next chapter, seeking, **Continue listening**, the heard tick and the daily limit of its tile. From the app you start it under **Listen**. Playtime and quiet times apply here too.

Audible delivers the book as a stream over the internet. Without internet the display hides the Audible entries until the connection is back; they stay in the app's library. A jump when seeking or continuing takes a moment, because the box requests the chapter again at the new place.

## Share listening position with Audible

With **Share listening position with Audible** (off by default, on the **Audible** page under **Playback**) the box keeps Audible up to date:

- Every 30 seconds, at a pause and when it stops, it reports where it is in the audiobook.
- If you start an audiobook anew on the box (not via **Continue listening**), it begins where the account left off – even if listening continued on the phone in between.
- Books heard to the end in Audible get the green heard tick on the box – within ten minutes.

If you parents listen to the same book in the same account, the box overwrites your place. Leave the switch off then.

> [!NOTE]
> If Audible no longer accepts the box's sign-in (the device was removed at Amazon, or it is a backup of another box), the **Audible** page says so and offers **Sign in again**.
