# Managing content

What is already on the box is managed on the **Library** page. Artist subscriptions and albums you added via **Search on Spotify** are also listed under **Library › Managed items**.

## Artist subscriptions

An **artist subscription** fetches all albums of an artist, such as a series of radio plays. A tap on a subscription opens its settings:

- **Episode from / Episode to**: only a range of episodes, for example 1 to 80. “Episode” is the position by release date, 1 is the oldest. Empty means open. **Apply range** saves the range.
- **Include/exclude albums** shows all albums of the artist with a switch. Switch off means: this album does not come onto the box. This applies at once.

**Remove subscription** cancels the subscription; the next sync takes the albums off the box.

## Individual albums

Albums added one by one are in the **Individual albums** list and can be removed there with **Remove**.

## Do not forget the backup

The list of content, the covers and the configuration are in the **configuration backup** ([Backup](../wartung/backup.md)). Make one before larger changes.
