# Podcasts and radio

Both land in the category **Radio & podcasts** (or another one, if you choose so when adding).

## Podcasts

There are three ways to add a podcast:

- **Search podcasts** (**Library › Search podcasts**): enter a search term, pick the podcast from the hits.
- **Paste link** with the type **Podcast (RSS)**: you enter the podcast's RSS address.
- **Spotify**: podcasts that exist on Spotify can be added like any Spotify content ([Spotify](spotify.md)).

When opened, the box shows the **episode list**, newest episode first.

### Listening progress and new episodes

For every episode the box remembers where it stopped and can mark new episodes. A dot on the podcast and on the episode shows what is new. A bar shows how far episodes that were started have been heard, a tick marks those heard to the end. All rules are on [Continue and “Now playing”](../bedienung/fortsetzen.md).

> [!NOTE]
> If the box cannot read a podcast's feed, the app says why instead of just showing “No episodes found”: server not found, reachable over IPv6 only (the box has no IPv6), not reachable, and the like.

## Radio

Radio stations come onto the box as a **stream address**:

- **Search radio stations** (**Library › Search radio stations**): enter a search term, pick the station.
- **Paste link** with the type **Radio stream**: you enter the address of the stream. It can also be a `.m3u` or `.pls` file: the box reads the stream address from it. As the **title** you enter the name of the station that appears on the display.

For radio the player shows **Live** instead of a progress bar. A radio station stops at once at the playtime limit, even if “finish the track” is set for tracks ([Daily limits](../spielzeit/tageslimits.md)).

> [!TIP]
> You can give a station's logo as the **cover**: enter the address of the picture when adding, or upload it ([Covers](cover.md)).
