# Covers

Under **Library › Cover** you manage the albums' pictures.

## Uploading your own cover

Upload a **square** picture (JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP, 300 to 1200 pixels). Afterwards you can enter the address of the uploaded picture for an item, for example for a radio stream that brings no picture of its own.

## Online covers

For albums without a picture of their own, from the NAS or from the memory card, the box can look the cover up online. The cover comes from **iTunes** or **Deezer**, but only for an unambiguous match.

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Search covers online** | switches the search on |
| **Also save as cover.jpg in the album folder** | puts found pictures in the folder, but only in folders without a picture. On the NAS the box needs write permission for this |
| **Search discarded ones again** | also searches albums again whose match you discarded |
| **Search all albums now** | starts the search for the whole library at once |
| **Search again without a match** | repeats the search only for albums where nothing was found |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For the search, the **folder names** are sent to Apple (iTunes) and Deezer. If you do not want that, leave **Search covers online** switched off.

### Wrong cover?

Under **Recently found** you see what the box found. A wrong cover can be **discarded**: the album then falls back to the picture of the folder above it.

## No pictures found

If an album stays without a picture, the box shows a **coloured card with the folder name** ([Covers and themes](../bedienung/cover-und-themes.md)). That is intended: the child recognises the entry by its name and colour, and no cover stays empty.
