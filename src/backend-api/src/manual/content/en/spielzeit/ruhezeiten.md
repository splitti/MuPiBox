# Quiet times

A **quiet time** is a time window in which the box does not play, for example for homework, the midday rest or the night. They are under **Playtime › Quiet times**.

## Creating a time window

1. Switch on **Quiet times active**.
2. Tap **+ Time window** and set the weekdays, the start time and the end time.
3. Give it a name under **Label**, for example “Homework” or “Good night”: it is shown on the display as the heading during this quiet time.

Time windows may go **past midnight** (for example 20:00 to 7:00). **Several windows** per weekday are possible, such as homework from 3 to 4 pm and bedtime from 8 pm.

## When a quiet time begins

Just like with the daily limit, you choose what happens when a quiet time starts: **Stop immediately**, **Play track to the end** or **Play album to the end**. **Keep playing for at most** also limits the extra time here.

During the quiet time the child sees a message on the display. The heading is the name of the time window; without a name, and for **Quiet now**, it says “Quiet time” (in the box’s language). You change the text below it under **Settings › Appearance › Text on the display** ([Playtime and quiet times](index.md)).

> [!TIP]
> If you need quiet or an exception from the quiet for once, use **Quiet now** or **Lift lock** ([Instant actions and sleep timer](schlaftimer.md)).
