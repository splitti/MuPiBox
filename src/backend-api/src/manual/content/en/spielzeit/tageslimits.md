# Daily limits

With daily limits you decide how many minutes may be listened to per weekday. They are under **Playtime › Daily limits**.

## Setting them

1. Switch on **Daily limits active**.
2. For every weekday enter the **minutes**. **0 means: blocked on that day.**
3. Decide when a new day begins (**Day changes at**): an hour from 0 to 23. With 4, for example, late listening after midnight still counts for the day before, and playback does not break off in the middle.

The limit counts the time during which playback actually happens.

## What happens at the limit?

The setting **When the limit is reached** decides:

| Choice | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Stop immediately** | playback ends on the spot |
| **Finish the track** | the current track is played to the end |
| **Finish the album** | the current album is played to the end |

So that an audiobook does not run on endlessly, **Keep playing at most** limits the extra time (5 minutes to 3 hours). After that the box also stops in the middle of a track or album.

> [!NOTE]
> **Podcasts** may finish the episode. **Radio** stops at once, because a radio station has no end.

## Exceptions

If you want to give more time for today or lift the block briefly, use the [instant actions](schlaftimer.md). They do not change the daily limit permanently.
