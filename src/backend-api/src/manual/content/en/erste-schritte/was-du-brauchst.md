# What you need

The MuPiBox runs on DietPi, a lean Linux for the Raspberry Pi. The **Raspberry Pi 3, 4 and 5** and the **Zero 2** are supported.

> [!NOTE]
> The project website keeps a complete, up-to-date shopping list and a list of compatible hardware: [What is needed?](https://mupibox.de/anleitungen/installationsanleitung/was-wird-benoetigt/) and [Compatible hardware](https://mupibox.de/anleitungen/installationsanleitung/kompatible-hardware/). This page only lists what the box's software expects from the hardware.

## Required

| Part | Purpose | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raspberry Pi | the box's computer | Pi 3, 4, 5 or Zero 2 |
| Memory card | system and your own media | the card also holds audiobooks and music; its free space is shown under **Settings › System › About the box** |
| Power supply | power | or a battery, see below |
| Touch display | operation by the child | the box's default resolution is 800 × 480 pixels ([Display](../hardware/display.md)) |
| Speaker and sound card | sound | the box knows many sound cards and amplifier boards, from the onboard output to the HiFiBerry DAC ([Sound and volume](../hardware/sound.md)) |
| Wi-Fi | Spotify, podcasts, radio, app | for your own files the box also works without internet |

## Optional

- **Battery with MuPiHAT**: makes the box portable and shows the charge level ([Battery, MuPiHAT and power button](../hardware/strom.md)).
- **Power button** (OnOff SHIM) with status LED: switches the box on and off cleanly ([Battery, MuPiHAT and power button](../hardware/strom.md)).
- **Rotary knob** for the volume ([Rotary knob and button](../hardware/drehregler.md)).
- **Fan**, controlled by temperature ([Fan](../hardware/luefter.md)).
- **Bluetooth headphones or speakers** ([Bluetooth](../hardware/bluetooth.md)).
- **WLED light strip** for light effects ([WLED](../hardware/wled.md)).
- **USB Wi-Fi stick**, if the built-in Wi-Fi is not enough ([Wi-Fi and LAN](../netzwerk/wlan-und-lan.md)).

## For setting up

- a **phone or PC** in the same network as the box, for the [app](app-oeffnen.md),
- the login details of your Wi-Fi,
- for Spotify a Spotify account and your own Spotify app on developer.spotify.com ([Spotify](../inhalte/spotify.md)).
