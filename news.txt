<h3>DEV 5.0.9 - changes compared to 5.0.8</h3>
<b>Themes</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Scroll bar below the covers, for every theme (wish of Andreas): under Appearance &gt; View one choice of three instead of the switch "Hide horizontal scroll bar" - Default (as the theme has it), Full width (across the whole width and thicker, with a larger touch area, easy to hit with big fingers) or Hide. The bar of the stage can now be dragged in both; the stage follows and reads the name aloud where it stops (when switched on).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Displays larger than 800 x 480 (e.g. the Raspberry Pi Touch Display 2 with 1280 x 720): with the resolution set in the app the page is drawn larger to match - sharp and with the same layout (1280 x 720: 1.5 times) - and covers come in a matching size. Not tested on such a display yet; feedback welcome.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The older plain themes (blue, green, red, pink, dark ...): under Appearance &gt; View the band behind the header and the panel behind the player's controls can be switched off, for the look of 5.0.7. Without the panel the controls of a light theme take the band's colour, so buttons and progress stay clearly visible; a dark theme keeps its own. Offered only for the themes that stay readable without them - not for the picture themes (suggested by toby_fischer).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Cover Flow theme, full screen (by Andreas): two fingers swiping up hide the top bar, swiping down shows it again; without it the covers get bigger (350 x 350 px, also in the player). Without the bar one finger from the bottom edge upwards goes one level back, two fingers from the right or left edge go to the next or previous category. Only in the theme "coverflow", and it can be switched off in the app under Appearance &gt; View (the switch is shown only while that theme is active). Zooming the display with two fingers is now off on every box (it was off in the debug mode only - children zoomed it by accident).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Cover Flow theme: one scrolling row for any number of entries (the special layouts for 1-3 and 4-10 entries are gone; with 3 covers or fewer the tilted ones still reach the edges of the screen), a missing cover shows a card with the name of the folder in a colour of its own (always the same per folder, also beside the track list), the player mirrors the cover below it, and the mirror of the lists reaches down to the bottom of the screen (by Andreas).</li>
</ul>
<b>Spotify</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Switching the box off (or a stop) with nothing playing on it no longer pauses Spotify on another device of the same account, a PC or a phone: the display paused its own Spotify player, and Spotify took that pause for the account's playback. It now pauses it only when the box itself plays (reported by hyperbit).</li>
</ul>
<b>App</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Settings pages of a group (Appearance: Theme, View, Start and maintenance images, Texts on the display ...) by Andreas: on a large screen a list 'On this page' beside the content with the pages of the group and the cards of the shown page; on a phone or tablet 'Previous page' and 'Next page' side by side at the end. Moving between the pages of a group is like changing tabs: the back arrow at the top (and the browser's back) leads up to the group, not page by page.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Player: the progress bar follows every report of the player (NAS, SD card, podcasts) - once the dot of a paused NAS album stood at the start while the time below it was right.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Library: tracks, albums and folders of the SD card or USB stick can be downloaded again (wish of hyperbit) - a track as it lies on the card, an album or a folder as a ZIP the box makes while it goes (not compressed: hardly any work for the Pi, no file in between); the folder shows the tracks and size of each album, a download over 1 GB asks first. Only with the app's login and only inside the media folders.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>About the box: the bar of the free space names the drive the box runs from (SD card, USB drive, SSD), and a USB stick or SSD running at USB 2.0 speed gets a hint - in a black USB 2.0 port it is many times slower than in a blue USB 3.0 port, the box starts slowly and stutters when memory runs short (found by hyperbit: a Pi with 1 GB stuttered at every start of Spotify until the stick moved to the blue port). On a Pi with less than 1.5 GB the display's cache in RAM is now at most 32 MB instead of 128 MB, memory such a box lacks.</li>
</ul>
<b>Audio</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Several sound outputs (by Andreas): next to the box's sound card the Raspberry Pi's 3.5 mm output, HDMI or a USB DAC are outputs of their own - in 'Listen with' on the display (one tile each, also without Bluetooth) and in the app's output row; the capsule in the player shows where it plays (speaker, headphones, Bluetooth).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Headphone jack next to the sound card: Settings &gt; Audio &gt; Sound card &gt; '3.5 mm output as well' keeps the board's 3.5 mm output on (DietPi switches it off with another card) - after a restart. The box's own card stays card 0, 'the box' is always the chosen card (PulseAudio took the jack as the default after the start, the speaker stayed silent), the chosen output is kept across a restart, a switch to the jack is never louder than the box was, and after a change of the sound card or the MuPiHAT the jack is switched on again. Switching it off puts DietPi's settings back.</li>
</ul>
<b>MuPiHAT</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Percent while charging and the time until full (by Andreas): while the pack charges, the voltage reads far too high, so the percent now comes from the charge that went in (from the percent before the cable), with the step to constant voltage as a known point; the app shows 'full in about ...', the battery profile has a capacity field for it.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Tested with a real charge on a weak 5 V input and made robust: a single false report of the charger after an I2C error, 'Done' or 'CV' far below the charge limit (the chip reports them when the input gives too little), no longer sets the percent to 99; a short pause without the cable goes on from what was reached, and the percent stays put while unplugged instead of showing the voltage that still reads the charge; the time until full goes by the current of the last quarter of an hour (playback takes part of the input) and the app's charger line follows the estimate. The end of a charge on a weak input is recognised too: the chip stops there straight out of constant current and the pack drops to 8.15 V at once, so the end counts when the charge was near the limit just before and the 'Done' stays for 5 minutes (or for half an hour above the recharge level); a top-up after it stays at full.</li>
</ul>
<b>Radio</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>A picture uploaded in the app and set as the cover of a radio station is shown on the display again (it showed the default picture, the browser had it - reported by boing86): an address of the box itself is taken from its cover folder instead of being fetched; other addresses of the home network stay refused.</li>
</ul>
<b>Installation</b>
</ul>

A fresh install no longer shows the DietPi website on the display (installing Chromium replaced the kiosk script; reported by Markus), and switching the onboard WiFi on again no longer removes the last line of config.txt.</li>

</ul>

<h3>DEV 5.0.7 - changes compared to 5.0.6</h3>

</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Local albums, NAS, podcasts and radio play with mpv instead of mplayer, which has hardly been maintained for years: streams reconnect after a drop, going on where you left off starts right there (no audible seek after the start), tracks follow each other without a gap, and the track number comes from the playlist itself. The update installs mpv; mplayer stays installed as a fallback - "playerEngine": "mplayer" in mupiboxconfig.json brings it back, and a box without mpv plays with mplayer.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>New: Settings › Audio › Volume › "Level the loudness" (soft or strong) - a quiet audio play is no longer quieter than the album before it. Changed while playing, without a break. Spotify plays through its own player and stays as it is.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The start sound, the low battery sound of the MuPiHAT and MP3 announcements play with mpv too.</li>
</ul>

</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Only one of cover and artist cover set: it is used for both - an entry without a cover of its own shows the artist's picture on its tile, in the resume list, in the player and at "Now playing" in the app (the artist tile already took the cover).</li>

A list made while Spotify blocked requests or a lookup failed is shown but not kept as complete - it is made again later instead of showing placeholders for hours. <b>Network</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The WiFi page shows the box's IPv6 address too, when it has one.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Network settings rearranged: WiFi now also holds its hardware (onboard WiFi, USB driver), the watchdog and its address (DHCP); the cable has a page of its own, LAN. "Network options" are gone. Cards pinned to the start page follow to their new place.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>"Get a new address" on the WiFi and on the LAN page renews only that connection's address (before: all of them were released, but only the WiFi was started again). The DHCP timeout is on both pages - one value for both.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The switch "Remote control via IP" is gone from the app: nothing read its value any more (the display always reaches the player on the box itself).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>A fixed IP address per saved WiFi network (the others keep DHCP), in the app and on the display. Before saving, a test checks it in the network the box is in, without dropping the connection: router and range, the address free, the router answering the new address (tried as a second address for a few seconds), the DNS server. The box switches at once, the WiFi connection stays. If it does not reach its router with the fixed address - right after saving or later in that network - it goes back to DHCP by itself and keeps the values paused to correct them; the app and the display say so.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>LAN for a PC plugged straight into the box: the router is optional for a fixed address (without one the internet stays with the WiFi).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The display no longer loses its WiFi symbol for good while the box is online: the online state in the network file is checked against the real one every 10 s and corrected (a write lost to the network file's own refresh left "offline" in it until the next start) (by Andreas).</li>
</ul>
