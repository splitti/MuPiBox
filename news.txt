<b>Player</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>A new playback starts unpaused and without the picture of the one before: after a pause, a title started next showed the play button on the display while it played, and a local album could show the picture of a podcast episode played before it.</li>
</ul>
<b>Web app</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Pinned cards on the start page: the parts of a whole card (e.g. the announcement templates and "Own text …") have the usual spacing.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The app is back in the update package: after the update to 5.0.5 the app (port 80, /app) was missing and stayed a white page.</li>
</ul>
<b>Installation and system</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The update stops before anything is replaced if its package lacks the app, instead of leaving the box without it.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The web server is restarted after the update and the installation instead of only reloaded, so the new certificate and the app on port 80 work at once and not only after the next restart.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>xdotool is installed: the display fills the whole screen and the goodbye picture shows in front of it.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The installation creates a simple certificate when the installed version has none of its own yet, so the web server starts.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The update restarts a running remote display (VNC), so it is reachable through the app only at once and not only after the next restart.</li>
</ul>
<h3>DEV 5.0.5 - changes compared to 5.0.4</h3>
<b>Highlights</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Choose the episodes of a podcast:</b> all, the newest or the first 10 / 20 / 50, or an own range – already when adding, with a preview of what ends up on the box.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Speech output in 21 languages:</b> Arabic, Chinese and Hindi now with Piper voices on the box, Japanese through Google.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>No more white screen after an update:</b> the display loads again by itself when the app did not come up.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Podcast servers in your own network</b> such as Pinepods or Audiobookshelf can be added: the web app asks once to allow the server.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Pin cards to the start page</b> – as a whole card (e.g. the charge curve or the announcements) or as a link.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Listen with:</b> switch between the box and Bluetooth headphones right on the display or in the web app.</li>
</ul>
<b>Podcasts</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Which episodes of a podcast go on the box: all, the newest 10 / 20 / 50, the first 10 / 20 / 50 or an own range (counted by date, episode 1 is the oldest), with the order (newest or oldest first). Set when adding from the podcast search or later when editing; a preview shows which episodes end up on the box.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The choice applies everywhere: the display, the episode lists of the web app, playing from the phone and the SD card, which can keep exactly the chosen episodes. The "new" dot only shows for chosen episodes.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Without internet the box shows the episodes saved on the SD card – those are the chosen ones already, the choice is not applied a second time.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Editing a podcast on the display keeps its choice of episodes and how many episodes stay on the SD card (the setting for the SD card used to get lost there).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Podcast servers in your own network (e.g. Pinepods, Audiobookshelf): the box fetches no addresses of the home network by itself, so nobody can use it as a detour to other devices. When a feed of the home network is added, the web app asks once whether to allow that server (host and port only); the list is under Settings &gt; Services &gt; Servers in the home network. The box itself and loopback addresses are never allowed.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Feeds whose address has its own query (e.g. ?api_key=…&amp;podcast_id=5, as Pinepods uses) are read completely on the display – before, everything from the "&amp;" on was lost and the display showed another feed.</li>
</ul>
<b>Listening and Bluetooth</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Listen with:</b> a tap on the volume at the top of the display's player chooses between the box's speaker and a paired Bluetooth device (headphones, speaker) - it connects the device, plays only there and keeps each output's own volume (a device connected the first time is not louder than the box). Not found: said on the display. The same choice is in the web app's "Now playing". Can be switched off for the display under Controls on the display. Only shown when a Bluetooth device is paired.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The box's sound card comes back by itself when it dropped out of PulseAudio (seen after headphones were disconnected: nothing was audible until a restart).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Bluetooth: the list of paired devices no longer shows devices a search found nearby (phones, watches and trackers without a name appeared there for a few minutes). "Connect automatically" finds the paired devices again on Debian 12 (bluetoothctl 5.66 no longer knows "paired-devices" - no device was connected at the start).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Bluetooth: removing a device no longer leaves Bluetooth switched off (it was switched off and at once on again, which failed), takes a moment instead of several seconds and shows at once that it is being removed.</li>
</ul>
<b>Speech output</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The box also speaks Arabic, Chinese, Hindi and Japanese (announcements, messages from the parents, names on the display), with texts of their own. Before, these languages fell back to German.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Only Piper voices the box can play are offered (some newer Chinese and Japanese voices need a newer Piper); without a voice the page says so, and Google speaks the language.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The page Appearance &gt; Read aloud is gone: the switch for reading names aloud is under Appearance &gt; View below the cover flow it belongs to, the language under Audio &gt; Speech output ("The box speaks").</li>
</ul>
<b>Web app</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Pin cards to the start page: every card with a heading has a pin. Some cards come as a whole card you can use right on the start page (charge curve, announcements, headphones), all others as a link that opens the page at that card. Order, card or link, and unpinning under Start &gt; Pinned &gt; Arrange. The pins are kept in the box, so every phone shows the same. Idea and first version by Andreas (Lippsson).</li>
</ul>
<b>Display</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>When the app has not come up 25 seconds after the page loaded (e.g. the box's server restarted during an update from the console), the display loads the page again – at most three times in five minutes.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Children's themes: the volume and the track number at the top of the player are on one line again (the track number sat 2 px lower).</li>
</ul>
<b>Installation and system</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>OnOff SHIM on Debian 13 "Trixie" (libgpiod 2): the power button and the power cut at shutdown work there too (pinctrl from raspberrypi-utils, installed where the system has it). Debian 12 "Bookworm" keeps the calls it always had.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The installation no longer calls DietPi's own installer for the DietPi-Dashboard: it fetched a nightly build from nightly.link, which is not always there (the installation failed with a 404), as a second dashboard on the same port. The dashboard is installed from its GitHub release as before; unzip is part of the package list now. Installation and update fetch a fixed version of the dashboard (v0.6.2, program and configuration of the same release, checked before it replaces the installed one): later releases split it into two programs with another configuration and could not break it this way.</li>
</ul>
<b>Legal</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The third-party notices also name Piper and its voices, which the box downloads itself when speech output is set up.</li>
</ul>
<h3>DEV 5.0.4 - changes compared to 5.0.3</h3>
<b>Highlights</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Find podcasts and radio stations in the web app:</b> one search across all podcast providers, including the ARD Audiothek (ARD Sounds), with a language filter and suggestions of popular kids' podcasts for each language; radio stations from radio-browser.info, also with suggestions.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Podcast episodes on the SD card:</b> per podcast the newest 1 to 20 episodes can be kept on the box, so they also play without internet.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Continue where you stopped:</b> every podcast episode continues at its last position, NAS albums too – without hearing the beginning first. Switchable, and how long a position is kept can be chosen.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>New episodes and progress on the display:</b> a dot marks new episodes, a bar shows how far an episode was heard and a tick marks finished ones (both switchable).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Gentle end:</b> sleep timer, end of the playtime and start of a quiet time fade the sound out over 20 seconds instead of stopping hard.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Box health at a glance</b> and a <b>weekly summary</b> in Telegram on Sunday evening.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Natural voices on the box:</b> Piper speaks offline with voices for all 17 languages – names on the display, announcements of the remaining playtime, bedtime and the end of the sleep timer, and announcements from the parents ("Dinner is ready!") from the web app or Telegram.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Evening dimming:</b> the display gets darker in the evening (and during quiet hours, if you like) and bright again in the morning.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Own volume limit for Bluetooth headphones</b>, and a <b>tidier web app</b>: settings pages in two columns without gaps, with more in them (charge curve, signed-in devices, test message …).</li>
</ul>
<b>Podcasts and radio</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Add content: one podcast search for all providers (Apple Podcasts directory and ARD Audiothek) in the language of your choice; only shows in that language are offered. Without a search term the page suggests popular kids' podcasts of that language.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>ARD Audiothek shows can be added as podcasts (ard:&lt;id&gt;); their episodes come straight from the ARD Audiothek.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Radio station search (radio-browser.info) with suggestions of kids' stations for the chosen language.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Podcast and radio search can be switched off under Settings &gt; Services; adding content only offers services that are set up (Spotify only when signed in).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Episodes on the SD card: per podcast "Off" or the newest 1, 3, 5, 10 or 20 episodes; single episodes can be saved or removed by hand. Saved episodes play without internet.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>ARD Audiothek: an episode is only saved when the ARD itself releases it for download (from the ARD's download address); all others are streamed only and show a lock. Saved ARD episodes are deleted when their time online at the ARD ends.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Every episode keeps its own position (Settings &gt; Controls on the display &gt; Podcasts): on, and kept for 1 month to unlimited. The app shows how far each episode was heard.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>New episodes are marked on the display and in the app for 3, 7 or 14 days (only episodes that appeared after the podcast was added; gone once played). Started episodes show a progress bar, finished ones a tick.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>More robust podcasts: very large feeds load, an episode keeps its identity when the feed changes its links, the length comes from the feed, and slow feeds no longer hold up the display.</li>
</ul>
<b>Player</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Continuing podcast episodes and NAS albums starts at the saved position without an audible beginning.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Long podcast episodes: the display no longer returns to the overview after pauses, and a dropped connection is opened again at the same position.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Sleep timer, end of the playtime and start of a quiet time fade out over 20 seconds; the volume is back afterwards (also when the sleep timer is stopped while fading).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Spotify: librespot is gone. The display's player is the box's device in the Spotify app on the phone (choose it under "Devices" and play from the phone); the update removes librespot and its cache, which frees up to several GB on the SD card. New installations get Widevine again, which the display's player needs.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Telegram "Report playback" now also reports Spotify tracks played on the display.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Spotify: the display's player could lose its connection to Spotify without noticing – it counted as ready, but every start went nowhere. It now checks with Spotify before a start and every 3 minutes and connects again when needed.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Volume: an own maximum while Bluetooth headphones or a speaker are connected (Settings &gt; Audio &gt; Volume); a louder box goes down to it as soon as they connect.</li>
</ul>
<b>Speech output</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>New page Settings &gt; Audio &gt; Speech output: Piper on the box (offline, recommended), Google (online) or silent. Without a Piper voice for the box's language Google or silence takes over, as chosen. The choice also applies to reading names aloud on the display.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Voices for all 17 languages (Piper voices from Hugging Face): listen to a sample on the phone, download, use, delete, and try any downloaded voice on the box itself. Quality, size and licence are shown; Piper itself is downloaded with the first voice.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The names of the library are prepared in the background (after choosing a voice and whenever the library changes), so the display reads them without delay.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Automatic announcements: the remaining playtime 2–15 minutes before its end, the start of a quiet time and the end of the sleep timer – with your own texts and a volume of their own (never above the box's volume).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Parents' announcements: templates and your own text, from the start page ("Announcement") or Telegram (/say text), with a gong, and playback paused and continued afterwards (or only quieter).</li>
</ul>
<b>Display</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Evening dimming (Settings &gt; Display): from / until, brightness in the evening, dimming gradually over up to 60 minutes, optionally also during quiet hours.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Own pictures for the start, the goodbye and the empty battery (Settings &gt; Appearance &gt; Boot screens &gt; "Own pictures"): a photo or logo from the phone or PC, fitted to the display (fill or show whole); the console takes its colour, so nothing flashes. The design stays for the maintenance screens and every picture without an own one.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Rotation as tiles, the usual resolutions to choose from, "Display off after" in fixed steps.</li>

<li id='linews' class='linews'>Themes Super Mario and Steampunk: new name fonts (Titan One, Rye) under a free licence. The unused initramfs splash screen is removed by the update.</li>
</ul>
<b>Web app</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Library: filter by source (Spotify, Smart Sync, podcasts, radio, SD card, NAS).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>iPhone/iPad: the web app on the home screen no longer loads itself again at every change of page ("Verbinde mit der Box …").</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>New page Settings &gt; System &gt; Box health: storage, temperature, power supply (under-voltage), SD card errors, memory, services, internet, NAS, Spotify sign-in, certificate and saved episodes – with what to do and a link to the right page when something is off.</li>

<h3>DEV 5.0.3 - changes compared to 5.0.2</h3>
<b>Highlights</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>The new MuPiBox web app</b> for phone, tablet and PC: play, library, playtime, Spotify, NAS and all settings in one place, in 17 languages – and on Android it can be installed like a real app.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Much faster box:</b> it starts and shuts down several seconds quicker, and it now keeps an index of all content – SD card, Spotify and NAS – so switching between categories and opening artists and albums is instant, with covers loading right away.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Search Spotify and add content from the web app:</b> find albums, audiobooks and artists and add them with one tap, or paste any Spotify, radio or podcast link.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Spotify Smart Sync:</b> manage the box from your own Spotify account – albums in playlists named after the box appear on it automatically, whole artists can be followed.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Upload media from the web app:</b> copy tracks and whole folders from the phone or PC straight onto the SD card, no network share needed.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Covers from the internet:</b> albums without a picture can get one automatically (iTunes / Deezer, switched on in the web app), and any cover can be searched online, replaced by your own picture or hidden.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>NAS in all tabs:</b> NAS folders can appear in Audiobooks, Music or Other, a folder index finds any folder instantly, and downloaded folders stay up to date by themselves.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Playtime limits and quiet hours:</b> daily listening time per weekday and quiet times (homework, bedtime), with a gentle end of the current track or album.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>30 new kids' themes</b> with mascots and big buttons, 16 boot screens with the box's own name, and display texts in 17 languages.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Parents' remote control:</b> Telegram commands (extra time, quiet now, sign-in link), a live picture of the display and remote control of the box right in the web app.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Secure connection:</b> the box's own certificate for https without warnings, one password for web app and admin interface, and many security fixes.</li>
</ul>
<b>New MuPiBox web app</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>New web app at http://&lt;box&gt;/app for phone, tablet and PC: now playing, listening, library, playtime, Spotify, NAS, covers, appearance, audio, battery, network, services, security and system in one place. http://&lt;box&gt;/ now opens a sign-in page where you choose between the web app (beta) and the admin interface.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>One password for the web app and the admin interface. With "Require sign-in" switched off both can be used in the home network without a password. You can also sign in with the QR code on the box (Settings &gt; Parent web app) or with the Telegram command /login.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>"Stay signed in" keeps a device signed in for 90 days, also across restarts. "Sign out all other devices" or a new password ends all other sessions, in the admin interface too.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Start page: what is playing now (cover, progress, controls, volume with the hearing protection limit marked), tiles for battery, listening time today, quiet hours and WiFi, quick actions (+15 min, Quiet now, Sleep timer) and notices only when needed (battery low or not charging, Spotify login running out, update available, MuPiBox news).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>17 languages (taken from the browser or chosen by hand), light and dark design, and it can be added to the home screen like an app.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The admin interface stays available; "Advanced settings" in the app opens it already signed in.</li>
</ul>
<b>Listen and play</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Listen page in the app: audiobooks, music, radio &amp; podcasts, folders on the SD card, NAS folders and whole Spotify artists as covers, with search and filters. Tap to play on the box (the app asks first if something else is playing). Podcasts show their episodes to choose from, newest first.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The display follows playback started from the app or Telegram: it opens the player with the right cover and switches when another album is started.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The player shows the cover stored in the file that is playing (MP3 and FLAC), so a folder of different stories shows a cover for each story.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Radio links to m3u / pls playlists play the first stream of the list. WMA files from the NAS play.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Resume jumps straight to the saved track and position without playing bits of other tracks first. The resume list is sorted by last played, and a position is only saved after something was really listened to.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Spotify and local / NAS / radio no longer play at the same time after switching.</li>
</ul>
<b>Library</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Library page: library entries, SD card folders and NAS folders in one list with search and filters for category and source, grouped by artist (artist &gt; album). Edit or delete entries and SD card albums, change the address of a radio station or podcast. The list updates itself when something changes elsewhere.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>"+ Add": search Spotify (add albums or a whole artist), paste a link (Spotify, radio, podcast, m3u / pls, with ID check, audiobook detection, sorting, shuffle and range) or upload tracks and whole folders from the phone or PC to the SD card.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Covers: search one online or use your own picture (cropped to a square), for entries, SD card folders and NAS folders (on the NAS it is saved as cover.jpg if the box may write there). "No cover" hides a folder's picture without deleting anything.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Online covers (off by default; app or Admin &gt; Cover): albums without a picture, and albums with a scanned, non-square picture, get a cover from iTunes or Deezer. Only covers that clearly match are used. They can also be saved as cover.jpg in the album folder.</li>
</ul>
<b>Spotify</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Smart sync (optional): create Spotify playlists whose names start with the box name (e.g. "MuPiBox Audiobooks", "MuPiBox Music"). The box adds their albums to its library and keeps them up to date. Conflicts with your own entries are shown in the app.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Managed content: follow a whole artist, optionally only a range of episodes, and leave out single albums.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>A step-by-step set-up in the app (client ID, client secret, redirect URI to copy). <b>Redirect URI:</b> boxes that are already set up keep https://&lt;box&gt;/spotify.php (it now forwards to the app). New set-ups use https://&lt;box&gt;/app/spotify-callback. The app shows which one must be in your Spotify developer app.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Spotify now keeps the login of a developer app for only 6 months. The box remembers the date, reminds you 14 and 3 days before (Telegram and app), and the start page takes you straight to a new login.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Spotify Connect: sign in again from the app, see its state and errors, or switch it off completely (it stays off after restarts and updates).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Faster and more robust: covers are cached on the box, artists with hundreds of albums load much faster, a long Spotify block ("too many requests") no longer hangs the lists, and playback started from the app or Telegram plays on the box.</li>
</ul>
<b>NAS</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>NAS folders can be shown in the Audiobooks, Music or Other tab instead of the NAS tab. A folder with several series can show each series as its own tile. Set in the app (NAS or library) or in the NAS tab of the admin interface.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>NAS page in the app: sign in, profiles, folder tree with search, show / hide / download folders, reload covers.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>HTTPS for the NAS, also with the NAS's self-signed certificate (confirm its fingerprint once).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Faster and more reliable: large folders open in a fraction of a second, the next track is loaded in advance (no gap between tracks), playback carries on where it stopped after a connection drop, and a slow or waking NAS is waited for.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Folders with their own tracks and subfolders can be opened. System folders (@eaDir, #recycle, ...) and folders without audio are hidden. WebP and JFIF covers work. New in the NAS tab: "Show only selected" and "Reload covers". Downloaded folders stay up to date (new episodes are added, deleted ones removed).</li>
</ul>
<b>Kids' themes and display</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>30 new kids' themes (e.g. Cuddle Moon, Unicorn Dream, Dino Land, Pirate Cove, Fire Station, Outer Space, Day &amp; Night), each with its own mascot, background and cover placeholder, large covers and big buttons.</li>
