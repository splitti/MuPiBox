<h3>DEV 5.0.7 - changes compared to 5.0.6</h3>
<b>Podcasts</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>When the box cannot read a podcast's feed, the app says why instead of "No episodes found": server not found, reachable over IPv6 only (the box has no IPv6), not reachable, a server in the home network that is not allowed yet (with a button to allow it), an error answer, or no feed at the address.</li>
</ul>
<b>Covers</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Only one of cover and artist cover set: it is used for both - an entry without a cover of its own shows the artist's picture on its tile, in the resume list, in the player and at "Now playing" in the app (the artist tile already took the cover).</li>
</ul>
<b>Display</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>A category with a big library shows what is there after a few seconds, the rest follows - before, it showed only loading dots until every Spotify entry was looked up (minutes after an update), and after a minute the page was even loaded again, which started it over.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The lists of the categories the box keeps survive an update (now in server/config, which the update keeps) - the first tap after an update no longer makes them from scratch.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Spotify: the box's cache of Spotify's answers (and of the covers) survives an update as well - every entry was asked for again after an update, with a big library minutes of loading and a long way into Spotify's rate limit. The cache holds 4000 answers now (1000 before: a big library threw out its own entries while being made). A list made while Spotify blocked requests or a lookup failed is shown but not kept as complete - it is made again later instead of showing placeholders for hours. A Spotify search entry fetches its first 100 hits (before: every page of a thousand, a hundred requests for one entry).</li>
</ul>
<b>Network</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The WiFi page shows the box's IPv6 address too, when it has one.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Network settings rearranged: WiFi now also holds its hardware (onboard WiFi, USB driver), the watchdog and its address (DHCP); the cable has a page of its own, LAN. "Network options" are gone. Cards pinned to the start page follow to their new place.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>"Get a new address" on the WiFi and on the LAN page renews only that connection's address (before: all of them were released, but only the WiFi was started again). The DHCP timeout is on both pages - one value for both.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The switch "Remote control via IP" is gone from the app: nothing read its value any more (the display always reaches the player on the box itself).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>A fixed IP address per saved WiFi network (the others keep DHCP), in the app and on the display. Before saving, a test checks it in the network the box is in, without dropping the connection: router and range, the address free, the router answering the new address (tried as a second address for a few seconds), the DNS server. The box switches at once, the WiFi connection stays. If it does not reach its router with the fixed address - right after saving or later in that network - it goes back to DHCP by itself and keeps the values paused to correct them; the app and the display say so.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>LAN for a PC plugged straight into the box: the router is optional for a fixed address (without one the internet stays with the WiFi).</li>
</ul>
<b>Display</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>"WiFi settings" is now "Network settings": WiFi or LAN chosen at the top (the LAN settings also without the cable in use), "Renew address" for WiFi and LAN, and "Address" for every saved WiFi network - DHCP or fixed, with an onscreen keypad and the test. A way back when the box cannot be reached over the network.</li>
</ul>
<b>Review fixes</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Security: the LAN settings check every address given, also under DHCP (an unchecked one could carry a line break and so a line of its own into /etc/network/interfaces); addresses of the box itself written as IPv6 (::ffff:7f00:1, as a URL parser writes 127.0.0.1) are recognised by the player's playlist check and the home-network feed check.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Spotify Smart Sync removes nothing from the library in a run in which a playlist, a pinned album or an artist could not be read (timeout, rate limit) - their entries and overrides stay, the next complete run tidies up.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Fixed WiFi addresses: saving from the app and the display at the same time no longer loses one of them; a network whose profile points at a missing address goes back to DHCP by itself.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Update: the box's programs stop before the user data is copied (a change saved meanwhile was replaced by the older copy); a backup of a restore that failed is kept under a dated name instead of being thrown away by the next update, and the failure is said at the end.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Second review round - files written in one piece: a power cut while the box wrote a podcast's feed cache, a cover, the day's playtime or the offline podcast list left half a file (an empty podcast, a cut-off picture, the day's count at zero, kept episodes deleted as strays). The battery log is only rewritten when something expired (2 MB to the card every hour before). Covers are touched for the cache's order at most once an hour.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Player: a second pause or play within a second (display and app, Telegram, an announcement) no longer toggles local playback twice; a Spotify pause that failed does not count as paused.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Security: a WiFi password with a quote in it is set as its key (worked out as wpa_passphrase does) instead of breaking the command; the NAS address cannot point at the box itself; file keys of the app's log viewer, JSON editor, shares and driver installer only accept their own names; a data.json that is not a list is reported by add/edit/delete instead of ending the server.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Network: WiFi names with umlauts are found again for a fixed address or a new password; the watchdog for a fixed address counts its misses also while DietPi's WiFi monitor restarts the WiFi in between (it never got to its third miss before); a fixed address saved while the box was in another network is not reported as "test passed"; after saving, the WiFi list shows the address at once; a second WiFi adapter in the same network does not keep the same fixed address; the sound card's driver is not re-bound while PulseAudio is restarting; the LAN settings keep several DNS servers (dietpi-config's way) and read "false" as off; the offline media lists are written with their text as it is (double spaces in titles were collapsed).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Update: an aborted update is reported as failed to the admin interface and to curl | bash too (not "Update finished"), and starts the idle shutdown again; the WiFi monitor is only restarted when it runs (one switched off stays off); the udev rules for the USB WiFi adapter and the LAN cable are installed for every version that has the script (only dev versions got them - on a release, the cable did not switch between LAN and WiFi).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Display: an artist's album list that came back short while Spotify blocked requests is loaded anew at the next tap instead of being kept for ten minutes; leaving a shuffle album within its first seconds no longer leaves shuffle on in Spotify (the next audiobook played in random order); the NAS tab gives up after 50 s when the NAS is asleep instead of making the page reload; the kept lists are read again when the backend was still starting; a category made again while the library changed is made for the new version. Spotify paused from elsewhere - the parents' phone over Spotify Connect, or a stall - in the middle of a track: the player stays on the display as paused and its play button goes on (before: back to the list after ten seconds, with a stop); only the end of the album goes back as before. And the end of an album goes back to the list even with Spotify's autoplay on (a setting of the account): Spotify then went straight on with tracks of other albums, which the display showed under the album's name; now the box stops them and shows the list again.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>App: when the start gets stuck at "Verbinde mit der Box …" (the box's backend busy or restarting, a request never answered), the screen says after 12 s which step it is at and offers "Neu laden" and "Zur Anmeldung"; a request the box does not answer within 15 s ends with a message instead of waiting for ever. If app.js itself does not load, the page says so after 20 s with a reload button.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Battery profiles in a sensible order in the app (the batteries first, "USB-C mode" and "Custom" last). A box without a battery (profile "USB-C mode", a power supply or a power bank on USB-C): the app says "mains power" instead of "0 %" and "battery nearly empty", the MuPiHAT page shows no charge curve and thresholds for it, and the display shows no battery icon even when a battery is fitted but this profile is chosen.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Resume list ("Weiterhören") on the display: a podcast episode started from the app left a resume entry without its feed (and with the artist of the last local album), and that one entry made the whole list come up empty. The display now builds such an entry from the library's feed entry, skips an entry without an id, and the box refuses to store entries nothing could be found by again.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Playback started from the app: the display shows a podcast episode's title (not the feed's name twice), "Weiterhören" is saved for albums and episodes started from the app or Telegram (it never was before), a stop from the app no longer overwrites the saved position with zeros, and the app's playback card no longer shows the category as the artist of a local album. A stop from the app, Telegram or the sleep timer now closes the display's player page at once (it stayed with a pause button - 100 s for a podcast, for good with an album - and went on saving "Weiterhören" with zeros).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Start-up: the WiFi is only restarted when the box got no address within 60 s - since the faster start the start-up script tore down every connection that was just being made (about 30 s more without network after each power-on).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Shutting down: whatever plays is stopped first, then the goodbye sound - the music went on, louder, under it (reported by hyperbit).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Spotify: a start when Spotify does not know the display's player (it lost its sign-in at start-up without network) lets the display sign in anew and plays then, about 8 s later instead of not at all. At the end of an album's last track the box stops itself, so Spotify's autoplay adds no other album or track any more. Playtime and quiet hours end at a song's end at once (the next track no longer starts and fades out).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Spotify: the display's check every 3 minutes whether Spotify still knows its player was refused by the box ("Refused GET /spotify/token" in the log) and never ran - a player Spotify had dropped now connects again by itself.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Local albums and episodes: the display goes back to its list a second after the end (it waited 10 s, looking stuck).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>LAN switch (app and display): shows whether the port is switched off - not whether a cable has a link. Without a cable it read "off" although nothing was switched off, and a switched-off port looked like a pulled cable. The cable has a line of its own now: connected, no cable, or port switched off.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>A big podcast feed (more than 2 MB) without a readable item list is no longer handed to the slow parser (it blocked the backend for a long time on a Pi 3). The m3u generator's lock keeper ends with its script (killed, it kept the library lock for ever). Piper's input stream closing early no longer ends the server.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>A fixed LAN address with its own DNS server applies that server now (there is no resolvconf on the box). Offline podcasts: a download that finished while the folder was tidied is no longer deleted, and two changes of the list at once no longer collide. A failed announcement no longer blocks every one after it, and the music comes back in any case. A feed's episode data (ARD download release, end date, moved files) is refreshed even when its first episode is unchanged.</li>
</ul>
<b>Web app</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Shares & remote access (Samba, FTP, VNC) moved from Settings › Network to Settings › Services, as the last block "Access to the box". Network shows the connection only: WiFi and LAN (suggested by hyperbit).</li>
</ul>
<b>Spotify</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Smart-Sync with a Spotify app created since February 2026: every run ended with INTERNAL_ERROR, so artist subscriptions and albums never came in and old entries were not removed. Spotify gives such apps a playlist's entries only at a new address (/items instead of /tracks) - the sync reads it there now, which works for older apps too. A playlist that cannot be read is skipped instead of ending the run, and artist pictures are fetched one by one where Spotify no longer allows several at once (reported by hyperbit).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Smart-Sync asks Spotify for pages it allows such apps: 50 playlist entries (was 100) and 10 albums of an artist (50 for older apps - switched after Spotify's first "Invalid limit"). An artist's album list is kept for 6 hours: the sync every 15 minutes no longer fetches all ~30 pages of a big artist (one with almost 300 albums) each time.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>When Spotify blocks the app's requests ("too many requests", 429), the sync stops at once instead of asking on for the next albums and artists, and waits for the time Spotify gives (also "sync now" in the app and the bot asks nothing before). The log says how long ("Spotify blocks this app's requests for N min (until …)"), and the app shows it as the next sync. A block while fetching artist pictures no longer applies the run without them.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The albums of an artist subscription are filed under the subscribed artist, also where Spotify names someone else first (a choir, a speaker or a publisher): they were filed under that other artist and missing from the subscribed one.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>A failed sync says why: the reason is in the log, in the app (Bibliothek › Spotify, "Grund") and in the bot's /syncstatus, and a run that could not read a playlist, an album or an artist names it ("Nicht gelesen").</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The player no longer writes three lines per file (title, path, tags - also for every announcement) into its log; they come with the debug log level only.</li>
</ul>

<b>Highlights</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>

</ul>
<b>Podcasts</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Which episodes of a podcast go on the box: all, the newest 10 / 20 / 50, the first 10 / 20 / 50 or an own range (counted by date, episode 1 is the oldest), with the order (newest or oldest first). Set when adding from the podcast search or later when editing; a preview shows which episodes end up on the box.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The choice applies everywhere: the display, the episode lists of the web app, playing from the phone and the SD card, which can keep exactly the chosen episodes. The "new" dot only shows for chosen episodes.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Without internet the box shows the episodes saved on the SD card – those are the chosen ones already, the choice is not applied a second time.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Editing a podcast on the display keeps its choice of episodes and how many episodes stay on the SD card (the setting for the SD card used to get lost there).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Podcast servers in your own network (e.g. Pinepods, Audiobookshelf): the box fetches no addresses of the home network by itself, so nobody can use it as a detour to other devices. When a feed of the home network is added, the web app asks once whether to allow that server (host and port only); the list is under Settings &gt; Services &gt; Servers in the home network. The box itself and loopback addresses are never allowed.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Feeds whose address has its own query (e.g. ?api_key=…&amp;podcast_id=5, as Pinepods uses) are read completely on the display – before, everything from the "&amp;" on was lost and the display showed another feed.</li>
</ul>
<b>Listening and Bluetooth</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Listen with:</b> a tap on the volume at the top of the display's player chooses between the box's speaker and a paired Bluetooth device (headphones, speaker) - it connects the device, plays only there and keeps each output's own volume (a device connected the first time is not louder than the box). Not found: said on the display. The same choice is in the web app's "Now playing". Can be switched off for the display under Controls on the display. Only shown when a Bluetooth device is paired.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>The box's sound card comes back by itself when it dropped out of PulseAudio (seen after headphones were disconnected: nothing was audible until a restart).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Bluetooth: the list of paired devices no longer shows devices a search found nearby (phones, watches and trackers without a name appeared there for a few minutes). "Connect automatically" finds the paired devices again on Debian 12 (bluetoothctl 5.66 no longer knows "paired-devices" - no device was connected at the start).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Bluetooth: removing a device no longer leaves Bluetooth switched off (it was switched off and at once on again, which failed), takes a moment instead of several seconds and shows at once that it is being removed.</li>
</ul>

<h3>DEV 5.0.4 - changes compared to 5.0.3</h3>
<b>Highlights</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Find podcasts and radio stations in the web app:</b> one search across all podcast providers, including the ARD Audiothek (ARD Sounds), with a language filter and suggestions of popular kids' podcasts for each language; radio stations from radio-browser.info, also with suggestions.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Podcast episodes on the SD card:</b> per podcast the newest 1 to 20 episodes can be kept on the box, so they also play without internet.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Continue where you stopped:</b> every podcast episode continues at its last position, NAS albums too – without hearing the beginning first. Switchable, and how long a position is kept can be chosen.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>New episodes and progress on the display:</b> a dot marks new episodes, a bar shows how far an episode was heard and a tick marks finished ones (both switchable).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Gentle end:</b> sleep timer, end of the playtime and start of a quiet time fade the sound out over 20 seconds instead of stopping hard.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Box health at a glance</b> and a <b>weekly summary</b> in Telegram on Sunday evening.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Natural voices on the box:</b> Piper speaks offline with voices for all 17 languages – names on the display, announcements of the remaining playtime, bedtime and the end of the sleep timer, and announcements from the parents ("Dinner is ready!") from the web app or Telegram.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Evening dimming:</b> the display gets darker in the evening (and during quiet hours, if you like) and bright again in the morning.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'><b>Own volume limit for Bluetooth headphones</b>, and a <b>tidier web app</b>: settings pages in two columns without gaps, with more in them (charge curve, signed-in devices, test message …).</li>
</ul>
<b>Podcasts and radio</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Add content: one podcast search for all providers (Apple Podcasts directory and ARD Audiothek) in the language of your choice; only shows in that language are offered. Without a search term the page suggests popular kids' podcasts of that language.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>ARD Audiothek shows can be added as podcasts (ard:&lt;id&gt;); their episodes come straight from the ARD Audiothek.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Radio station search (radio-browser.info) with suggestions of kids' stations for the chosen language.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Podcast and radio search can be switched off under Settings &gt; Services; adding content only offers services that are set up (Spotify only when signed in).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Episodes on the SD card: per podcast "Off" or the newest 1, 3, 5, 10 or 20 episodes; single episodes can be saved or removed by hand. Saved episodes play without internet.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>ARD Audiothek: an episode is only saved when the ARD itself releases it for download (from the ARD's download address); all others are streamed only and show a lock. Saved ARD episodes are deleted when their time online at the ARD ends.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Every episode keeps its own position (Settings &gt; Controls on the display &gt; Podcasts): on, and kept for 1 month to unlimited. The app shows how far each episode was heard.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>New episodes are marked on the display and in the app for 3, 7 or 14 days (only episodes that appeared after the podcast was added; gone once played). Started episodes show a progress bar, finished ones a tick.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>More robust podcasts: very large feeds load, an episode keeps its identity when the feed changes its links, the length comes from the feed, and slow feeds no longer hold up the display.</li>
</ul>
<b>Player</b>
</ul>
<ul id='ulnews' class='ulnews'>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Continuing podcast episodes and NAS albums starts at the saved position without an audible beginning.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Long podcast episodes: the display no longer returns to the overview after pauses, and a dropped connection is opened again at the same position.</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Sleep timer, end of the playtime and start of a quiet time fade out over 20 seconds; the volume is back afterwards (also when the sleep timer is stopped while fading).</li>
<li id='linews' class='linews'>Spotify: librespot is gone. The display's player is the box's device in the Spotify app on the phone (choose it under "Devices" and play from the phone); the update removes librespot and its cache, which frees up to several GB on the SD card.
