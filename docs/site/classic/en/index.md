**MuPiBox Classic** is the previous generation of the music player. Its installation, administration and controls differ from MuPiBox NG. Do **not** follow NG instructions for a Classic box.

## Read the Classic guide

The current complete instructions are still available at [mupibox.de – Classic guides](https://mupibox.de/anleitungen/classic/). The external guide may not be available in all languages.

## Coming to this site

Dedicated Classic pages for installation, setup, music providers, hardware and maintenance will later be published under `docs.mupibox.de/classic/en/`. NG instructions under `/ng/` will stay separate.

## Using MuPiBox NG?

See the [MuPiBox NG guide](https://docs.mupibox.de/ng/en/).
